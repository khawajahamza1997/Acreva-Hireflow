## Step 5 — Canva mein edit (45–60 min)

### Project banao

1. Canva → **Create a design** → **Video** → **1920 × 1080**
2. Name: `Fiverr Intro - Acreva`

### Timeline (exact order)

| Track | Clip | Length (trim) |
|-------|------|----------------|
| 1 | `face-hook.mp4` | ~12 sec (full) |
| 2 | `clip-upload.mp4` | ~6 sec |
| 3 | `clip-scores.mp4` | ~7 sec |
| 4 | `clip-outreach.mp4` | ~7 sec |
| 5 | `clip-gmail.mp4` | ~8 sec |
| 6 | `face-cta.mp4` | ~10 sec |

**Total target: 45–55 seconds.** Agar lamba ho to upload/scores clips chhote karo — **Gmail mat kaato**.

### Audio

1. Face clips ki apni awaaz rakho (mute mat karo agar clear ho)
2. Screen section par **voiceover** lagao — original screen audio **mute** ya low
3. Optional: Canva **Audio** → soft corporate music, volume **15%**

### Text overlays (optional, 2–3 max)

- Scores par: `AI scores candidates`
- Gmail par: `Real email — not a mockup`

**Mat likho:** email, phone, website URL (Fiverr reject kar sakta hai)

### Transitions

Sirf **Dissolve** — 3–4 jagah. Har cut par fancy animation mat lagao.

### Captions

Canva → **Captions** → auto generate → fix spelling: **HireFlow**, **Sarah Chen**, **Fiverr**

### Export

- **Share → Download → MP4 Video**
- Quality: **1080p**
- Save as: `fiverr-intro/export/acreva-intro.mp4`
