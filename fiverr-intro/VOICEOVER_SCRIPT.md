# Fiverr Intro — Voiceover Script (Scene by Scene)

**Total screen voiceover:** 56 seconds  
**Pace:** Thora slow bolo — ~130 words per minute (calm, clear)  
**Record:** Har scene alag file, ya ek continuous track timeline ke saath  

---

## Recording tips

1. **Quiet room** — fan AC band, phone silent  
2. **Phone / mic** 6–8 inch munh se door  
3. Har sentence ke baad **half-second pause**  
4. Pehle **normal speed** mein padho — agar scene short ho to thora slow, agar long ho to thora tez  
5. Canva mein har scene ki clip ke **exact length** par voiceover trim karo  

---

## Scene 1 — Onboarding (12 sec)

**Screen par:** Job title type karo → JD paste → Continue → green banner  

**Bolo (12 sec — slow pace):**

> I open Acreva HireFlow and start a new role from scratch.  
> I type the job title — Senior React Developer — paste the full job description, click continue —  
> and within seconds the position is live and ready for candidate uploads.

**Words:** ~38 | **Target:** 12 sec  

**Agar 3 sec mein khatam ho raha ho:** tum tez bol rahe ho — **50% slow** karo, ya LONG line use karo (upar trimmed section).
